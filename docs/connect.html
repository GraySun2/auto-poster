autopost

Sends your finished short videos to your own TikTok drafts. You add the caption and post them yourself in the TikTok app.

Connect your TikTok account

autopost asks for two permissions:

  1. Your basic profile (name and picture), to show which account is connected.
  2. Upload videos to your drafts. Each video waits in your TikTok inbox. Nothing is posted until you open it, write the caption and tap Post.

Sign in with TikTok

You can remove access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services.

Open the sign-in link from your repository

Run Actions → Autopost connect with app tiktok-link, then open the link on the run's summary page. It brings you back here with everything filled in.
